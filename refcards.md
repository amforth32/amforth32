Reference cards provide an exhaustive list of words available in a particular build and target. They are generated automatically as one of the build artifacts. The words are grouped based on predefined list of categories (`core/dev/categories`), listed as links at the top of the card for quick navigation. Few synthetic categories for words that are not in the `categories` list are:

* MCU - words from the MCU directory
* Uncategorized - everything else

The legend is included at the end of the reference card.
